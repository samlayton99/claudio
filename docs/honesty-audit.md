# Honesty Audit

The gate question answered without sycophancy, recorded so future-Sam can't renegotiate with himself. (2026-06-11)

## Verdict

**Build it — conditionally.** The conditions are load-bearing, not decorative.

## The against-case, at full strength

1. **The meta-trap.** Building productivity systems is the highest-grade procrastination available to someone with ADHD and these skills. It feels like work, produces artifacts, serves the dopamine loop. Two predecessor systems are dead; the base rate applies. The most likely failure is not bad design — it's an unbounded build that displaces research, PROD, and family, then rots the first month attention lapses. The design question is settled; the **discipline question is the open one**.
2. **Partial commoditization is coming.** Functions 1–3 (ingest, distill, serve) are what every platform is racing toward; within ~18 months much of that plumbing comes free with the assistants. If claudio were only that, the right move would be to wait.
3. **Opportunity cost is real**: Hazy Lab, PROD, church, marriage. At 5–8 focused hrs/week post-push, a justified bet; at 20, it eats what it serves.

## Why yes anyway

1. **The purpose contract is the uncommoditizable core.** No platform will let you own the canonical statement of what your life is for, audit every automation against it, point competing vendors' agents at it, and carry it across decades of tool churn. "Life purpose as a typecheck against reality" is the product; nobody is building it. It also serves faith and family first — the owner's own test.
2. **The pain is real, recurring, and his** — and the v1 dashboard's reflection loop demonstrably worked on him before the system ossified.
3. **Marginal cost from here is low**: spec done, mini plugged in, thinking finished.
4. **Failure has salvage value**: a clean life database, a wiki, an eval corpus — durable assets even if the automation layer dies.

## The three conditions (standing gates in the build plan)

1. **P2 in two weeks.** Capture-by-text + morning brief paying daily within two weeks of first DDL, or shrink before continuing.
2. **Value at every stopping point.** Every phase leaves the user better off if work stops there forever.
3. **The kill criterion.** At P3 exit: weekly maintenance minutes > weekly minutes saved ⇒ stop or shrink. Agreed to in advance, while sober.

## Build-order consequence

**Front-load the irreplaceable.** The purpose contract and the mirror agent move earlier; replaceable plumbing moves later — the ecosystem keeps making plumbing cheaper, and nobody makes your purpose contract for you.

## Standing correction posture

Where the owner's review and first principles conflicted, pushback was given and stands: handshake is a protocol not a resident agent (capability is **issued, never declared**); approval acts stay on claudio-owned surfaces while approved work is handed off; the master password is verified by a hash, never an agent; atoms-as-summaries is a conceded risk managed by verbatim anchors + raw re-grounding, not eliminated.
