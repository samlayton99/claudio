## l1.atoms

Bias toward thoughtful, larger chunks; never per-message. Examples: a meeting; a thread-day; an idea (kind=idea); an evening of TV (the record is honest). Depth scales by destination (P8): summary -> +detail -> +quotes -> +wiki page.
