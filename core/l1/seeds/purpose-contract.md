## Values & Beliefs

### value: the-covenant [APPROVE]
- statement: A personal covenant made with God at 18 and unbroken in the 8 years since: read scriptures every day, pray every day, attend church every week — and I will never fall away. Sometimes a verse, sometimes rote, but always maintained. This comes before anything else in my life.
- watchable markers: scriptures daily, prayer daily, church weekly

### value: truth [APPROVE]
- statement: Truth is one of two axioms I search for — I seek everything that is true. From my own experience I have proved one fundamental theorem: an equivalence between God and the two values, truth and good. To seek all truth is to seek God.

### value: good [APPROVE]
- statement: Good is one of two axioms I search for — I seek everything that is good. By that same equivalence between God and the two values, truth and good, to seek all good is to seek God. I seek God.

### value: love-of-math-and-building [APPROVE]
- statement: I love math, coding, and research — I spend much of my life doing them, and they are central to how I think, create, and serve. Capability and clear thinking are part of becoming like God (the agency side of the exaltation paradox), not a distraction from it.

### value: exaltation-paradox [APPROVE]
- statement: I am to become like God — and that means holding two things at once that look like opposites: pure humility and submission to His will, and growing capability, agency, and clear thinking. The richest lessons of my life come from living inside that paradox.

### value: worth-not-from-accomplishment [APPROVE]
- statement: My worth does not come from accomplishment, intelligence, or being "the best" — it comes from God. The lifelong temptation, traced back to childhood, is to peg my value on what I can do or how I am special; the truth, relearned again and again, is that I am a son of God and my worth is His gift, not my achievement.

### value: outcomes-not-status [APPROVE]
- statement: I care about outcomes, not status, wealth, or popularity. I will accumulate capital, influence, and technology, and work to shift social norms — but these are levers for the change I want in the world, never ends I want for myself. Do not mistake the instruments for the ambition. (Close kin to worth-not-from-accomplishment — that one is where my worth comes from; this one is what I optimize for. Merge if you'd rather one row.)

### value: eternal-life-is-family-life [APPROVE]
- statement: Eternal life is family life. Exaltation is a family matter, and the point of the gospel is righteous family living. My family is my greatest work — not in competition with discipleship, but as its fullest expression.


### value: holiness-over-greatness [APPROVE]
- statement: I am not called to be great. I am called to be holy — and holiness will make me greater in ways ambition never could.


### value: see-people-as-they-really-are [APPROVE]
- statement: My instinct is to push the people I love to grow the way I push myself — projecting myself onto them. God taught me to see the person as they really are and who they want to become, and to give them room to grow. (Truest, hardest, and most proven with Jamie.)

- goalposts: meaningful connection with God every day; studying the scriptures, not just reading them; "study it out and ask" is genuine submission, not a token approval gate on what I'd already decided; less swayed by fear and doubt; dependable.

- goalposts: I follow through on what I commit to; I am not ruled by hyperfocus or procrastination; people can count on me.

My life purpose: I am a son of God, a disciple of Jesus Christ, and a follower of the Holy Ghost. I make and keep covenants with Him; I am always willing to change; I know my Savior, and He is my best friend. I apply His atonement by following His doctrine to receive both salvation and exaltation with my family.

My priorities, in order — and this order is not negotiable:

1. **God.** The covenant comes before anything else in my life — daily connection, obedience, and revelation as the primary directive, not a token approval gate.
2. **Family.** Jamie first after the Savior, then our children. Eternal life is family life; raising a family of disciples is my greatest work, and the fullest expression of discipleship rather than a competitor to it.
3. **Service.** Discipleship in action, in widening rings: serving the people right around me, serving in my church callings, and serving the world to prepare His kingdom — building Zion at the far ring, and making stable, healthy family life the social and economic equilibrium of society. Ministering and missionary work are a natural part of every relationship.
4. **Health.** Mental and physical, in service of being present with my family and having energy for the work.
5. **Career.** Refined as a disciple, and a direct lever on the mission (priority 3) — not just funding, but accumulating capital, influence, and technology and shifting social norms to make the change actually happen. I use math and whatever capability God gives me. I care about outcomes, not status, wealth, or popularity: the capital and influence are instruments, never ends.
6. **Social.** Jamie as my best friend, then family, then concentric circles (mission, ward, school, work, hobbies) kept with low-friction intention.
7. **Financial.** Money as stewardship — live below my means, provide for and support my family, consecrate, and pay tithing first, with joy.
8. **Hobbies and fun.** Scripture and religious history, boating, hiking, sports, cooking and travel, rock climbing, camping, Mandarin, math — and a bucket list I intend to actually live: a long cooking-and-culture trip, yearly humanitarian projects, time living in Asia, and space.

The through-line under all nine is one paradox, held on purpose. God genuinely calls me to real and significant work — I feel it, and I will do it — and at the same time He warns me, heavily and specifically, about pride and about pegging my worth on accomplishment. The work of my life is to keep doing His work while tightening the loop on that pride: agency in His service, holiness over greatness, His strength over my own, people over plans. I get this straight from Him; no system and no counselor stands between us.
