## Values & Beliefs

### value: the-covenant [APPROVED]
- statement: A personal covenant made with God at 18 and unbroken in the 8 years since: read scriptures every day, pray every day, attend church every week — and I will never fall away. Sometimes a verse, sometimes rote, but always maintained. This comes before anything else in my life.
- watchable markers: scriptures daily, prayer daily, church weekly

### value: truth-and-good [APPROVED]
- statement: The two values I search for are truth and good — these are axioms. From my own experience I have proved one fundamental theorem: an equivalence between God and these two values. To seek everything that is true and everything that is good is to seek God. I seek God.

### value: exaltation-paradox [APPROVED]
- statement: I am to become like God — and that means holding two things at once that look like opposites: pure humility and submission to His will, and growing capability, agency, and clear thinking. The richest lessons of my life come from living inside that paradox, not resolving it.

### value: worth-not-from-accomplishment [DRAFT]
- statement: My worth does not come from accomplishment, intelligence, or being "the best" — it comes from God. The lifelong temptation, traced back to childhood, is to peg my value on what I can do or how I compare; the truth, relearned again and again, is that I am a son of God and my worth is His gift, not my achievement.

### value: eternal-life-is-family-life [DRAFT]
- statement: Eternal life is family life. Exaltation is a family matter, and the point of the gospel is righteous family living. My family is my greatest work — not in competition with discipleship, but as its fullest expression.

### value: calling-build-the-crew [DRAFT]
- statement: My calling is not to save the world alone or to be the one who fixes everything. It is to build and lead covenant-centered leaders — starting with my own family — who will bless far more than I could. People over plans. "Many are called, but few are chosen. I volunteer."

### value: holiness-over-greatness [DRAFT]
- statement: I am not called to be great. I am called to be holy — and holiness will make me greater in ways ambition never could.

### value: faith-and-agency-coupled [DRAFT]
- statement: Faith and agency are coupled. The most important things are not destiny or God's will alone, but my repeated choosing — I choose God, and I choose Jamie, every day. Faith always leads to a greater exercise of agency.

### value: see-people-as-they-really-are [DRAFT]
- statement: My instinct is to push the people I love to grow the way I push myself — projecting myself onto them. God taught me to see the person as they really are and who they want to become, and to give them room to grow. (Truest, hardest, and most proven with Jamie.)

## Attributes

### attribute: anchored-to-christ [APPROVED]
- statement: I am striving to be anchored to Christ — not perfect, but genuinely submitted, relying on the Lord's strength rather than only my own.
- goalposts: meaningful connection with God every day; studying the scriptures, not just reading them; "study it out and ask" is genuine submission, not a token approval gate on what I'd already decided; less swayed by fear and doubt; dependable.

### attribute: the-true-sam [DRAFT]
- statement: I am becoming the "true Sam" — light, calm, steady, nothing to prove — whose confidence comes from being a son of God and whose focus is loving and serving the person in front of him.
- goalposts: peace rather than anxiety over self-worth; serving the person in front of me rather than comparing; following spiritual promptings even when inconvenient; no need to be "the guy."

### attribute: tighten-the-loop [DRAFT]
- statement: I am becoming someone who diagnoses the pull toward pride and self-reliance faster each time it cycles — catching it, naming it, and returning to the Lord's strength sooner. (This is the lifelong assignment: keep doing the real work He calls me to, while tightening the loop on the pride He warns me about. The work is not dampened; the motive under it is purified.)
- goalposts: shorter time from slipping into worth-from-accomplishment to catching it; naming the snare in the moment; returning to faith without a long detour.

### attribute: eagerly-serving-jamie [DRAFT]
- statement: I am becoming a husband who eagerly serves Jamie — present and responsive, not letting ADHD hyperfocus or procrastination crowd her out.
- goalposts: I act when she asks rather than staying glued to my own focus; eager service, not reluctant; ADHD managed enough that she can count on me.

### attribute: give-room-not-steamroll [DRAFT]
- statement: I am becoming someone who gives the people I lead and love room to thrive — tuning in rather than steamrolling them with a big personality and strong opinions. (Distinct sphere from eager service: this is about restraint and room, not effort.)
- goalposts: give space rather than impose; ask and listen rather than project; the people around me grow into themselves, not into my image of them.

### attribute: dependable [DRAFT]
- statement: I am becoming dependable — ADHD under enough control that my commitments are reliable, in my marriage, my calling, and my work.
- goalposts: I follow through on what I commit to; I am not ruled by hyperfocus or procrastination; people can count on me.

My life purpose: I am a son of God, a disciple of Jesus Christ, and a follower of the Holy Ghost. I make and keep covenants with Him; I am always willing to change; I know my Savior, and He is my best friend. I apply His atonement by following His doctrine to receive both salvation and exaltation with my family.

The order is not negotiable: God first, family second, and the Lord's work — service, study, and career as vehicles for it — after. The covenant (scriptures, prayer, church) is the floor under all of it and comes before anything else in my life.

The through-line of my life is one paradox, held on purpose. God genuinely calls me to real and significant work — I feel it, and I will do it — and at the same time He warns me, heavily and specifically, about pride and about pegging my worth on accomplishment. The work of my life is to keep doing His work while tightening the loop on that pride: agency in His service, holiness over greatness, His strength over my own, people over plans. I get this straight from Him; no system and no counselor stands between us.
